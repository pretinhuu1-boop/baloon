## Prompt (English — optimized for AI video generation)

Cinematic slow-motion sports footage of a soccer ball striking a metal crossbar at night in an empty professional stadium.

**CAMERA & LENS**
Camera starts at a low-angle wide shot from behind the kicker's feet, showing the vast empty stadium stretching out. As the ball launches, the camera smoothly transitions via invisible cut to a tight tracking shot following the ball's arc through the air. Final frames: extreme close-up macro of the ball compressing against the crossbar metal, then bouncing off with visible vibration ripples on the bar.

Anamorphic lens with subtle horizontal flares. Shallow depth of field (f/1.4 equivalent) — the ball stays razor-sharp while the stadium seats dissolve into creamy bokeh. Slight barrel distortion at wide moments for immersive scale.

**LIGHTING**
Single dominant backlight: a powerful golden-amber stadium spotlight positioned high and behind the goal, creating a dramatic rim light around the ball and goalposts. The light source is warm (3200K–3500K), casting long golden rays with visible volumetric light through atmospheric haze.

Secondary fill: cool blue moonlight (6500K) from camera-left, very subtle, just enough to separate shadows. Creates a gold-vs-blue complementary contrast.

Practical lights: distant stadium flood lights in the deep background, slightly overexposed, creating starburst bokeh points. The crossbar has a faint metallic reflection of the golden spotlight.

**COLOR PALETTE**
Deep blacks (#0A0F0A) dominating 60% of the frame. Rich gold/amber (#D4A54A) as the hero accent color in the spotlight and rim light. Muted green (#1A472A) barely visible on the grass, desaturated to keep focus on the gold-and-black contrast. No bright whites — the brightest element is the spotlight flare at ~80% luminance.

Overall grade: high-contrast, crushed blacks, lifted gold midtones. Think David Fincher meets Nike commercial — moody, premium, powerful.

**ATMOSPHERE & PARTICLES**
Light atmospheric haze filling the stadium air, making the spotlight beams volumetric and visible. Fine dust particles floating in the beam path, catching the golden light. As the ball hits the crossbar: micro water droplets or dew explodes off the ball surface in slow motion, catching the backlight like tiny golden sparks.

The grass is slightly dewy — not wet, just enough for a subtle sheen under the stadium light.

**MOTION & TEMPO**
0s–2s: Wide establishing shot, camera barely drifting forward. The stadium feels colossal and empty. Tension building.
2s–4s: The ball enters frame from bottom, launched upward. Camera tracks it smoothly, following its parabolic arc. Speed ramps from 120fps to 240fps as the ball approaches the crossbar.
4s–6s: IMPACT. Extreme slow motion (480fps equivalent). The ball deforms against the cylindrical crossbar. Metal vibrates. Droplets scatter. The golden backlight explodes into a lens flare bloom at the moment of contact. At the exact frame of impact, a shockwave of golden energy radiates outward from the contact point — concentric rings of warm light expanding through the atmospheric haze like a sonic boom made visible. The scattered dew droplets become part of this golden shockwave, tracing luminous arcs. The entire frame briefly bathes in an intense golden bloom — this is the "million-dollar moment," the visual peak that says "you just unlocked something extraordinary."
6s–8s: The golden shockwave dissipates into floating particles of warm light that drift slowly like embers. Ball bounces away. Camera holds on the vibrating crossbar — which now has a faint residual golden glow along its surface, fading like cooling metal. Then slowly pulls back to reveal the empty stadium. The golden spotlight returns to its normal intensity, and the floating embers settle into darkness. The scene returns to elegant stillness — but the air feels different, charged, as if something monumental just happened.

**COMPOSITION & FRAMING**
0s–2s (Wide): Rule of thirds — the crossbar sits on the upper horizontal third. The golden spotlight is positioned at the upper-left power point. The lower two-thirds are dominated by negative space (dark grass, empty field), creating a sense of vastness and anticipation. Leading lines: the field markings and stadium seat rows converge toward the goal, pulling the eye to the crossbar.

2s–4s (Tracking): The ball occupies the center-right third as it arcs. The camera tilts to keep it on the rising diagonal — bottom-left to upper-right — the most dynamic compositional line. Negative space above the ball creates room for it to "travel into."

4s–6s (Impact): Centered composition — the ball and crossbar meet at dead center for maximum visual weight. The frame-within-a-frame effect: the goalposts create a natural rectangular frame around the impact point. The golden flare bloom breaks the symmetry asymmetrically to the right.

6s–8s (Pull-back): Wide symmetrical composition. The goal is centered, the stadium wraps symmetrically around it. The golden light source returns to the upper-left power point. Heavy negative space in the lower frame — elegant, breathing room.

**DEPTH STAGING (FG / MG / BG)**
- **Foreground** (0–5m): Dewy grass blades, slightly out of focus, catching golden light as tiny specular highlights. In the tracking shot, the ball occupies this plane.
- **Midground** (5–30m): The goal, crossbar, and net. This is the stage — sharp focus during impact. The goalposts are vertical anchors framing the action.
- **Background** (30m+): Stadium seats rising into darkness. Flood lights as overexposed starburst points. Deep bokeh. The stands are empty — implying scale without distraction. A faint atmospheric haze separates midground from background.

**POV & NARRATIVE PERSPECTIVE**
The camera is an omniscient observer — not a participant. It's the eye of destiny watching the ball's journey. There's no human POV (no over-shoulder, no first-person). The perspective is godlike: the camera moves in ways no human eye could (tracking a ball at 480fps, holding steady during macro impact). This creates a sense of fate and inevitability — the ball was always going to hit that crossbar.

The viewer should feel like they're watching a mythological event, not a sports replay.

**ART DIRECTION & PROPS**
- **The ball**: Classic white panels with black pentagons. Slightly worn — not brand new, not destroyed. A few scuff marks. The kind of ball you'd find in any park in the world. No brand logos.
- **The crossbar**: Round aluminum tube, slightly weathered. Not rusty, but not pristine — it has the patina of thousands of games. A few scratches catch the golden light.
- **The net**: White hexagonal mesh, hanging loosely behind the crossbar. It sways slightly from ambient wind. Not taut — relaxed, organic.
- **The stadium**: Modern but anonymous. Dark seats (charcoal/navy). No team colors, no banners, no flags. The architecture suggests a 40,000+ capacity venue. Clean lines.
- **The grass**: Manicured but not artificial. Natural imperfections — a few slightly longer patches. Deep green under the golden light reads as dark olive-emerald.

**TEXTURE & MATERIAL DETAIL**
- **Ball surface**: The synthetic leather panels have a visible micro-texture — tiny hexagonal dimples that catch the rim light as individual specular points. During the impact macro, we see the seams compress and the panel surface deform slightly.
- **Crossbar metal**: Brushed aluminum with a satin finish. Micro-scratches visible in the extreme close-up. When the ball hits, the metal vibrates — and the vibration is visible as a slight blur ripple traveling along the bar.
- **Dew droplets**: Each droplet is a tiny golden sphere in the backlight. They shatter into micro-particles on impact — some become golden streaks (motion blur), others hang in the air as perfect spheres.
- **Grass blades**: Individual blades visible in foreground bokeh — translucent when backlit, showing the cellular structure as a golden glow.

**EYE PATH & VISUAL WEIGHT**
Frame-by-frame viewer eye journey:
- 0s: Eye enters at the golden spotlight (brightest point, upper-left) → travels down the light beam → lands on the crossbar
- 2s: Eye follows the ball (highest contrast object — white against dark) along its arc, bottom-left to upper-right
- 4s: Eye is locked center on the impact. The flare bloom pulls attention slightly right, then the scattered droplets create a radial explosion pattern that keeps the eye centered
- 6s: Eye follows the ball bouncing away (movement attracts), then settles on the vibrating crossbar (residual motion)
- 8s: Eye returns to the golden spotlight as the camera pulls back, completing the visual circle

The golden light is the anchor throughout — the eye always knows where "home" is.

**MOOD & INTENTION**
Epic solitude. One person vs. the challenge. The empty stadium is not sad — it's an arena waiting for a hero. The golden light is aspirational, premium, like a trophy's glow. The slow-motion impact should feel POWERFUL — this is not just a kick, it's a million-dollar moment.

The video must feel like a luxury sports brand ad crossed with a cinematic movie trailer. Every frame should be wallpaper-worthy.
