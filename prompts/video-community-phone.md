## Prompt (English — optimized for AI video generation)

Cinematic close-up of a person's hands holding a smartphone horizontally, filming a soccer ball kick toward a goal at golden hour in a raw urban environment.

**CAMERA & LENS**
The camera starts as an extreme close-up on the smartphone screen — we see the phone's camera interface displaying a small soccer goal in the distance. Slowly, the camera racks focus and pulls back (dolly out) to reveal the full scene: hands holding the phone, arms extended, with the real environment unfolding behind the device.

The final composition frames the phone as a portal: the phone screen shows the recorded perspective while the real world expands around it in beautiful bokeh. Think "screen within a screen" — the phone is the hero object at the intersection of digital and physical.

50mm prime lens equivalent, f/2.0. The phone stays tack-sharp as the center of attention. Background melts into a warm, creamy bokeh of golden-hour light, blurred grass, and a distant improvised goal.

**LIGHTING**
Golden hour magic hour — the sun is 5–10° above the horizon, directly behind the scene. This creates:
- A powerful warm backlight (2800K–3200K) that wraps around the phone edges and the person's hands with a golden rim glow
- The phone screen is self-lit, showing the camera interface with a slight cool tone (contrast against the warm environment)
- Lens flare: one elegant horizontal anamorphic flare streaks across the frame as the sun peeks from behind the phone — gold and amber tones
- Grass in the foreground catches the low sun, creating tiny golden light dots throughout the lower third

No artificial lighting. Everything is natural, warm, authentic.

**COLOR PALETTE**
Warm amber and gold (#D4A54A, #F5D78E) dominating 70% of the frame through the backlight. The phone itself is dark (black device, #0A0F0A edges), creating a strong silhouette contrast. The phone screen emits a cooler white-blue light, creating a subtle complementary tension with the warm environment. Grass is a desaturated warm green (#2A4A2A) — not vivid, just enough to ground the scene.

Grade: warm highlights pushed toward amber, shadows toward deep olive-black. Skin tones on the hands are warm and golden. Think iPhone commercial meets Terrence Malick golden hour.

**ENVIRONMENT & SETTING**
An informal, authentic setting — NOT a professional stadium. This could be:
- A dirt field in a Brazilian favela with an improvised goal made of sticks
- A beach at sunset with shoes marking the goal posts
- A backyard with a rusty small goal

The key message is ACCESSIBILITY — you don't need a stadium, you don't need expensive gear. Just a phone, a ball, and any goal. The environment should feel real, lived-in, warm, aspirational in its simplicity.

In the deep background (out of focus): a soccer ball mid-flight, heading toward the improvised goal. It's a golden silhouette against the sunset — just a shape, not detailed. The action is implied, not explicit.

**ATMOSPHERE & PARTICLES**
Golden-hour dust particles floating in the air, caught by the low backlight. They look like tiny golden fireflies. Thin grass seeds or dandelion-like particles drifting slowly across the frame.

The air has that warm, thick quality of late afternoon — slightly hazy, slightly glowing. Heat shimmer is NOT present (too much distortion), but the air feels warm and dense with light.

**MOTION & TEMPO**
0s–2s: Extreme close-up on the phone screen. We see a camera app interface, slightly out of focus. The frame is almost abstract — dark edges of the phone, glowing screen. Slow drift forward.
2s–4s: Focus rack — the phone screen goes soft, the camera pulls back to reveal hands holding the phone. The golden-hour environment blooms into view behind and around the device. Beautiful bokeh transition.
4s–6s: The full "portal" composition is revealed. Phone centered, hands steady, the real world expanding in warm bokeh all around. A soccer ball is visible mid-arc in the deep background. The sun peeks from behind the phone, triggering a gentle lens flare that streaks horizontally.
6s–8s: Slow push-in back toward the phone. The screen now shows the ball hitting the goal (the moment captured) — and at this exact moment, the phone screen BLOOMS with an intense golden-white flash, as if the capture itself triggered something powerful. A pulse of warm golden light radiates outward from the phone screen into the real environment — the phone becomes a source of light, briefly illuminating the hands and nearby grass with a warm glow that wasn't there before. The hands subtly react — a micro-celebration. The golden light intensifies as the sun dips lower. The phone screen settles from the flash back to the camera interface, but now with a warmer, more golden tint — as if the device absorbed the golden-hour magic. Frame ends with the phone screen glowing against the darkening warm sky, the screen's light now matching the sunset perfectly — digital and natural gold becoming one.

Everything at 60fps, smooth and fluid. No jarring cuts — one continuous elegant camera move.

**COMPOSITION & FRAMING**
0s–2s (Extreme CU): The phone screen fills 80% of the frame, slightly off-center to the right (rule of thirds — screen on right vertical third). The dark phone bezels create a natural frame-within-a-frame. Negative space on the left is pure darkness — mysterious, abstract.

2s–4s (Rack + Dolly out): As the camera pulls back, the phone migrates to dead center — breaking the rule of thirds intentionally for a symmetric "portal" composition. The hands create a natural V-shape pointing upward, framing the phone like a pedestal. Leading lines: the forearms converge toward the phone, pulling the viewer's eye to the screen.

4s–6s (Full reveal): Golden ratio spiral — the phone sits at the golden ratio point (slightly left of center). The sun flare enters at the upper-right, creating a diagonal energy line from upper-right to lower-left. The improvised goal in the deep background aligns with the left vertical third. The grass foreground occupies the bottom 20% — a natural base.

6s–8s (Push-in): The phone grows to fill 60% of the frame again. The screen becomes the compositional anchor — the viewer's world narrows back to this single device. Vignetting intensifies at the edges, naturally framing the phone.

**DEPTH STAGING (FG / MG / BG)**
- **Foreground** (0–1m): Wild grass tips and small wildflowers, heavily blurred (f/2.0 bokeh). They catch golden-hour light as warm, soft orbs in the lower 15% of the frame. A few blades cross the bottom edge, grounding the shot.
- **Midground** (1–3m): The hands and phone — the hero plane. Tack-sharp. The phone is the only object in critical focus. Skin texture on the knuckles is visible. The phone's glass surface reflects a tiny sliver of the sunset.
- **Background** (3m+): The "world" — heavily diffused. The improvised goal is a geometric shape (two vertical posts, one horizontal bar) rendered in warm bokeh. A soccer ball is a small golden circle mid-flight. The horizon line is low (bottom third), maximizing the golden sky above. Trees or buildings at the far edge are dark silhouettes.

**POV & NARRATIVE PERSPECTIVE**
The camera is a close companion — intimate, almost conspiratorial. It's the perspective of a friend standing next to you, looking over your shoulder at your phone, then stepping back to see the full picture. It's NOT a drone shot (too distant), NOT a GoPro (too chaotic), NOT a security camera (too cold).

This is a HUMAN perspective with cinematic polish. The camera breathes slightly — imperceptible micro-movements that feel organic, not robotic. The focus rack mimics how the human eye shifts attention from screen to environment.

The viewer should feel: "I could be holding that phone. That's MY hand. That's MY backyard."

**ART DIRECTION & PROPS**
- **The phone**: Modern smartphone, ~6.5" screen. Matte black frame (no shiny chrome — it would create distracting reflections). Generic design — could be any Android or iPhone from a distance. No visible brand logo. The screen shows a minimal camera interface: a record button (red dot), a timer, and the viewfinder showing the goal in the distance. The screen brightness is moderate — not blindingly white.
- **The hands**: Natural, unmanicured. Could belong to a teenager, a father, anyone. A few natural creases and a hint of dust — these are hands that just picked up a ball from the dirt. No jewelry, no nail polish, no tattoos (maximum universality).
- **The improvised goal**: Two wooden sticks (~1.5m tall) driven into the ground, connected by a horizontal stick or rope at the top. Slightly crooked — charmingly imperfect. The wood is sun-bleached and weathered. This is NOT a store-bought goal.
- **The ball**: Visible in the background as a dark sphere with golden rim light. Slightly deflated or scuffed — a ball that's been kicked a thousand times. It's mid-flight, heading toward the improvised goal.
- **The ground**: Packed brown dirt with patches of wild grass. Not a manicured field — a vacant lot, a schoolyard, a backyard. A few small stones visible near the foreground grass.

**TEXTURE & MATERIAL DETAIL**
- **Phone glass**: The screen has a faint fingerprint smudge visible at a glancing angle — one tiny detail that makes it feel real and personal. The glass edge catches the sunset as a razor-thin golden line.
- **Skin**: Warm, golden-lit. The fine hairs on the forearms are backlit, creating a soft golden fuzz. The knuckles have slight texture — this is not a lotion ad, these are working hands.
- **Grass**: Wild and varied — different heights, some dried golden blades mixed with green. A few seed heads catching the light as translucent golden puffs.
- **Wooden goal posts**: Visible grain pattern in the sun-bleached wood. A few tiny splinters at the top where the horizontal stick is lashed. The rope or wire connecting them has a rough, twine-like texture.
- **Air quality**: The golden-hour atmosphere has a visible density — not fog, but that thick, honeyed quality of late-afternoon light where you can almost "see" the warmth.

**EYE PATH & VISUAL WEIGHT**
Frame-by-frame viewer eye journey:
- 0s: Eye enters at the phone screen (brightest element, highest contrast against dark bezels) → reads the camera interface → notices the small goal in the viewfinder
- 2s: Eye follows the focus rack — as the screen blurs, attention shifts to the hands (new sharpest element). The golden environment blooming into view pulls the eye outward toward the edges
- 4s: Eye settles on the phone (center of gravity), then the lens flare draws it upper-right. The ball in the background catches peripheral attention — movement attracts. The eye bounces: phone → ball → sun → back to phone
- 6s: Eye tracks the push-in movement, narrowing back to the phone. The screen shows the ball hitting the goal — the eye reads this "story within a story"
- 8s: Eye rests on the glowing phone screen surrounded by warm darkness — the final image burned into memory

The phone is ALWAYS the visual anchor. Every other element exists to frame it, point toward it, or contrast against it.

**MOOD & INTENTION**
Empowerment through simplicity. The message is: "This phone in your hands is your ticket to a million." The golden hour light makes everything feel aspirational but ACCESSIBLE — this isn't a luxury shoot in a studio, it's magic happening in an ordinary backyard.

The phone is the bridge between the ordinary (backyard, dirt field) and the extraordinary (the million-dollar challenge). The warm, golden tone makes even the simplest setting feel premium and cinematic.

Emotional arc: curiosity (close-up mystery) → revelation (pull-back) → aspiration (the captured moment) → intimacy (push back in).

**SOUND DESIGN DIRECTION** (for reference, not generated)
Ambient golden-hour sounds: distant birds, soft wind through grass. A subtle warm synth pad building underneath. At 6s, a soft "UI capture" sound as the phone records the goal. Muffled celebration — authentic, not produced.

**TECHNICAL NOTES**
- The phone should be a modern smartphone (generic design, no specific brand logos visible)
- Phone screen should show a realistic camera interface — not a specific app, just a camera viewfinder
- Person's hands/arms only — no face visible (keeps it universal, "anyone can be this person")
